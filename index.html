LAB 07 — CONTAINMENT BREACH

Nexonyx Biotech, Sublevel 4. 03:14.

Dr. Alden Voss ran gene-splicing trials on an unlisted subject — logged only as "Subject 07" — without clearance. Forty minutes ago the containment pod cracked. Power failed. Comms went dark. You're the only technician still on-site.

Restore power, stabilize the sample, and reach the containment override before contamination seals the room for good. The incident board on the west wall has hints if you get stuck — but the real clues are scattered through the lab.

HOW TO PLAY
• WASD to walk. Move the mouse to look. Click glowing things to use them.
• When you click a note, a panel or anything that opens a window, your mouse is freed so you can use it. When you close it, click once on the room to look around again.
• Almost nothing here is just decoration. Most things either tell the story or help you solve a puzzle.
• Remember: the periodic table is your friend!
• Press N for your notebook: write notes and re-read clues.
• Stuck? Press H for an optional hint. Hints only appear when you ask.
• Take your time. You have plenty of it.

CONTAINMENT LOST

Contamination reached 100%. The outer door seals automatically. Whatever Voss was hiding down here, it stays hidden — and so do you.

CONTAINMENT RESTORED

You punch in the final code and the exit door hisses open. Cold air and daylight pour in. Behind you, the hallway lights die one by one. Whatever Voss made is still out there — but you are free.

"SUBJECT 07 RECOGNISED."

The lights fade to amber as the containment lasers shut down, not in failure but in acknowledgement. The system isn't unlocking the door for you — Subject 07 is.

A hidden tag flashes across a terminal: PROFILE MATCH: DONOR-LINEAGE. Subject 07 was stabilised using a genomic scaffold taken from one technician — you.

Every puzzle you solved was the lab confirming your identity. The door was never meant to keep you out — only everyone else.

IT'S ALIVE

The sample you just sequenced has woken up.
The gene splice brought Subject 07 back to life — and it has escaped into the vents.

Calm the lab before the contamination spreads.
